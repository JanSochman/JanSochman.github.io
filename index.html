Jan Šochman

I am a researcher at Centre for Machine Perception at Czech Technical University in Prague.

I finished my Ph.D. in 2009 on the topic of Learning for sequential classification advised by prof. Jiří Matas. I spent a year and half as a postdoc at the Computer Vision Group, University of Leeds, UK, where I worked with prof. David Hogg on the SUBITO European project. I have been part of several other EU projects since and I spent more than ten years cooperating with Toyota Motor Europe.

Current research interests

  • continual reinforcement learning
  • learning for dynamical systems
  • out-of-distribution detection
  • biologically inspired learning

Teaching

  • Pattern Recognition (en/cz)
  • Advanced Deep Learning
